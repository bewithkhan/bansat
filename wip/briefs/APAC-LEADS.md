# Asia-Pacific batch: entries and leads (from memory; every lead must be checked against an official source)

Writers follow BRIEF-MEA.md (its rules apply to this batch too; only the jurisdictions differ).

## East Asia: cn, hk, jp, kr
- cn: PIPL (2021) and the CAC; standard contract measures (2023); Provisions on Promoting and Regulating Cross-border
  Data Flows (March 2024); Network Data Security Management Regulations (from 1 January 2025); compliance audit
  measures (from 1 May 2025); AI-generated content labelling measures (from 1 September 2025); generative AI interim
  measures (2023); Cybersecurity Law amendments (2025); minors' online protection regulations (2024); under-14s'
  data is sensitive and needs a guardian's consent.
- hk: PDPO (Cap. 486) and the PCPD; direct marketing (Part 6A); doxxing amendments (2021); breach notification not
  mandatory unless amended; section 33 (transfers) never brought into force; any 2025-2026 amendment bill.
- jp: APPI and the PPC; the triennial review and any 2025-2026 amendment bill; the AI Promotion Act (2025); the
  Telecommunications Business Act's external transmission rules (cookies, 2023); the specified e-mail law (spam).
- kr: PIPA (2023 amendments, fully in force March 2024) and the PIPC; the AI Basic Act (in force 22 January 2026?);
  the Network Act; under-14s need a legal representative's consent; breach notice within 72 hours; the domestic
  representative rule.

## South Asia and Singapore: in, pk, sg
- in: DPDP Act 2023; the DPDP Rules 2025 and their staged commencement; the Data Protection Board; consent managers;
  verifiable parental consent for under-18s; the IT Act s.43A and SPDI Rules until superseded; CERT-In's 6-hour
  incident reporting directions (2022).
- pk: no comprehensive law unless one passed in 2025-2026 (check the Personal Data Protection Bill's status); PECA
  2016 and its 2025 amendments; PTA rules on unsolicited messages; sector rules (State Bank). If there's no law, say
  so plainly in each field and cite what does apply.
- sg: PDPA 2012 (amended 2020) and the PDPC; Do Not Call; breach notice to the PDPC within 3 calendar days; advisory
  guidelines on children's data (2024) and on AI (2024); the Online Safety (Relief and Accountability) Act 2025.

## Southeast Asia: id, vn, th
- id: Law 27/2022 (PDP), in force since October 2024; its implementing government regulation (draft or issued?);
  the supervising agency (formed or not); Komdigi; GR 71/2019; breach notice within 3 x 24 hours; children.
- vn: the Personal Data Protection Law (Law 91/2025/QH15, in force 1 January 2026?) replacing Decree 13/2023, and its
  implementing decree; the Ministry of Public Security (A05); the Law on Data (from 1 July 2025); the Cybersecurity
  Law; any 2025-2026 AI or digital technology law.
- th: PDPA B.E. 2562 (fully in force 1 June 2022) and the PDPC; notifications on breach (72 hours), DPOs and
  cross-border transfers; enforcement and fines since 2024.

## Malaysia, the Philippines and Oceania: my, ph, au, nz
- my: PDPA 2010 as amended in 2024 (staged from January to June 2025: DPOs, breach notification, portability,
  processors' security duty, higher fines); the PDP Commissioner's guidelines; cross-border transfer guidelines;
  the Online Safety Act 2025.
- ph: the Data Privacy Act 2012 and the NPC; its IRR and circulars (breach notice within 72 hours; registration);
  children (NPC advisories); the SIM Registration Act.
- au: Privacy Act 1988 and the APPs; the Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024 (statutory tort from June
  2025, automated-decision transparency from December 2026, the Children's Online Privacy Code); the OAIC; the
  Notifiable Data Breaches scheme; the Spam Act 2003; the social media minimum age (under-16s, from 10 December 2025).
- nz: Privacy Act 2020 and the Privacy Commissioner; the Biometric Processing Privacy Code 2025; IPP 3A (notice for
  indirect collection, from 1 May 2026); the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007; breach notice as soon as
  practicable.
