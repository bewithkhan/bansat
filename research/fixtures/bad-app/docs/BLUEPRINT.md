# Blueprint (written, never built)

Row Level Security is enabled on every table. Year of birth is asked at sign-up, with a parent or guardian's email for
under-13s. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link and List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers. The
Content-Security-Policy, X-Content-Type-Options and Referrer-Policy headers are set. Analytics load with
loadWhenConsented. Users can Delete my account and Download my data. The privacy notice names Supabase, OpenAI,
Resend, Stripe, Google Analytics and Hotjar.
